# Blueprint: Michels Travel - The Sovereign Diaspora Infrastructure

## 1. Vision Statement
To transition from a transactional travel agency to a **Sovereign Life-Event Hub** for the Brazilian, American, and Hispanic diaspora. The goal is to eliminate cross-border friction (legal, financial, and logistical) through the application of the Canonical Laws of Software Engineering.

---

## 2. Strategic Pillars (Tooling Roadmap)

### Pillar A: Sovereign Identity & Multi-National Documentation
**Objective:** Solve the "Dual-Passport Paradox" and immigration uncertainty.
*   **Tool 1: Dual-Passport Routing Engine.** An intelligent check-in module that assigns the correct passport to each flight leg (e.g., Brazilian for GRU arrival, American for MIA arrival) to ensure seamless immigration.
*   **Tool 2: Immigration Radar.** Automated monitoring of Visa (B1/B2), ESTA, and reciprocity changes between USA, Brazil, and LATAM countries.
*   **Tool 3: Consular Bridge.** Direct API integration (where available) or automated guidance for passport renewals and consular emergencies.

### Pillar B: Cross-Border Financial Sovereignty (FinTech Layer)
**Objective:** Eliminate the 6-7% loss in currency exchange and international card fees.
*   **Tool 4: The Diaspora Pay Gate.** Native support for local payment methods across borders.
    *   **From USA:** Integration with Zelle and Apple Pay.
    *   **From Brazil:** Full Pix integration for family tickets purchased by residents abroad.
*   **Tool 5: Tax-Compliant Expense Reporting.** Automated generation of travel expense reports formatted for both IRS (USA) and Receita Federal (Brazil), critical for business owners in the diaspora.

### Pillar C: Predictive Concierge (AI Disruption Management)
**Objective:** Human-centric protection against airline inefficiency.
*   **Tool 6: Disruption Oracle.** Using historical flight data to predict missed connections in hubs like JFK, MIA, or PTY. Offers re-routing options *before* the airline sends an official alert.
*   **Tool 7: Automatic Claim Generator (Sovereign Defense).** If a flight is delayed >3h, the system automatically drafts the legal claim document based on ANAC 400 or EU261, ready for the user to submit.

### Pillar D: Family Reunion Logistics
**Objective:** Simplify the "Family Visit" workflow.
*   **Tool 8: The Family Cloud.** A shared space where a user in the US can manage documentation and flight preferences for parents or relatives in Brazil. Allows "Remote Check-in" where the son/daughter in the US completes the process for the senior traveler in Brazil.
